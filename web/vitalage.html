    Scientific basis

    Blood age is Phenotypic Age, developed by Levine et al. (2018) from US NHANES III and validated in 11,432 adults by Liu et al. (2018). The published coefficients are used unchanged. It has not been validated for Indian populations.

      Fitness Age converts published mortality hazard ratios into years with the same rate PhenoAge uses. It is a literature-based score, not a validated clock.

        VitalAge is an independent implementation and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the authors cited. It is not a diagnostic tool; talk to a doctor about any result that worries you. Source code: github.com/rawmatterx/vitalage
