Scientific basis

Internal age is a blood score built to track DNA-methylation aging: in NHANES 1999-2002 adults aged 50-84 it correlated 0.49 with DNA internal age (GrimAge2) and 0.49 with DNA pace of aging (DunedinPoAm), beating PhenoAge on a locked test set (research/RESULTS.md). It is an estimate, not a DNA test. Pace of aging is how fast internal age moves per calendar year across repeat tests.

PhenoAge, shown in the tables, is Phenotypic Age, developed by Levine et al. (2018) on US NHANES III data and validated in NHANES IV by Liu et al. (2018). The published coefficients are used unchanged. It has not been validated for Indian populations.

    Fitness Age combines effects from separate meta-analyses; it is a literature-based score, not a validated clock.

      VitalAge is an independent implementation, not affiliated with or endorsed by the authors cited. Not a diagnostic tool.
