Export for the analyst

One file with every enrolled participant's research data, in the vitalage-pilot-v1 format that the analysis code reads.

What is in it

  • The random study code, birth year, sex and smoking.
  • Blood markers (in standard units), internal age, PhenoAge and VitalAge for each test.
  • Monthly band medians and the band model; DNA-age results.
  • Every date as days since the person's first blood test.

No names, emails, birth dates or calendar dates. People who withdrew are not in it. Every download is logged.
